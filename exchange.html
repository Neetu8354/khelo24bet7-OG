What is a Betting Exchange?

A betting exchange is a peer-to-peer marketplace where you place bets directly against other players instead of a traditional bookmaker. On the Kheloo24 Exchange, you choose your own odds, accept offers from other members, or set the price you want. This often leads to better value and more control over every bet you place.

Think of it like a stock market for sports. Buyers (backers) and sellers (layers) trade positions on the outcome of an event. The exchange takes a small commission on winning bets, not a built-in margin, so the odds stay closer to the true market.

Back Betting

Back betting is the familiar way of betting: you are supporting an outcome to happen. For example, if you back Team India to win a cricket match, you win when India wins. The more confidence other users have in that result, the more likely your bet is to be matched quickly.

You can set your own odds. If another player agrees to take the opposite side, your bet is matched instantly. If no one matches your price, your bet remains unmatched until someone accepts it or the event starts.

Lay Betting

Lay betting is the opposite of a traditional bet. You take on the role of the bookmaker and bet against an outcome. If you lay Team India, you win if the team loses or the match is a draw, depending on the market rules.

Your potential profit is the stake of the backer, while your liability is the amount you have to pay out if the backed result happens. Laying can be a powerful way to lock in profit or trade out of a position before an event finishes.

How Exchange Works

Using the Kheloo24 Exchange is simple once you understand the four main steps:

  1. Pick a market from cricket, football, tennis, basketball, horse racing or other sports.
  2. Choose your odds and decide whether to back (for) or lay (against) a selection.
  3. Enter your stake and submit the bet to the exchange.
  4. Your bet is matched when another player accepts the opposite side of the wager. After the event settles, the winner receives the profit minus a small commission.

Market Liquidity

Liquidity is the amount of money waiting to be matched in a market. High liquidity means there are plenty of back and lay bets available, so your orders get matched quickly and at better prices. Popular events like the IPL, international cricket, major football leagues and tennis Grand Slams usually have the strongest liquidity.

If a market has low liquidity, your unmatched bet may sit longer. You can improve your chances by offering more attractive odds or waiting for more players to join the market closer to the start time.

Live Markets

Kheloo24 Exchange offers in-play or live betting markets that update as the action happens. Prices move in real time based on what is taking place on the field, court or track. This lets you react to wickets, goals, red cards and set points as they occur.

Live markets are ideal if you follow a sport closely and can read momentum changes. You can back and lay during the same match, giving you the chance to trade your position for a guaranteed profit or to cut a loss.

Cash Out

Cash Out lets you settle all or part of your bet before the event is over. If your selection is winning, you can bank a profit early. If the match is turning against you, Cash Out can help you recover some of your stake before the final result is known.

This feature is useful in fast-moving sports like cricket and football where a single moment can change the likely outcome. It puts you in control, so you are not locked into the full risk of the bet.

Important Terms

Back – a bet that an outcome will happen.
Lay – a bet that an outcome will not happen.
Matched bet – a bet that has been accepted by another player.
Unmatched bet – a bet waiting to be accepted at the odds you requested.
Liquidity – the amount of money available in a market to be matched.
Liability – the maximum you can lose when laying a bet.
Commission – the small fee charged on net winnings for using the exchange.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Kheloo24 Exchange different from a sportsbook?
Yes. A sportsbook sets the odds and takes your bet. On the exchange, you bet against other players, which usually gives better odds and the ability to lay outcomes.

Can I place both back and lay bets?
Absolutely. You can back a team to win or lay it to lose. Many users combine both to trade their positions during live events.

What happens if my bet is not matched?
An unmatched bet stays open until another player accepts it, the odds move in your favour, or the event starts and the bet is cancelled.

Which sports have the best liquidity?
Cricket, football and tennis generally have the most active markets and fastest bet matching.

How is commission calculated?
Commission is charged only on net winnings from a market, not on your total stake. The rate is displayed in the rules section of the exchange.
