Exchange

Real-time betting exchange platform with competitive odds

What is Exchange?

Our Exchange platform allows you to bet against other players rather than against the house. This peer-to-peer betting system offers better odds and more flexibility in your betting strategy.

Key Features

• Better Odds: Get competitive odds from other players
• Lay Betting: Bet against outcomes, not just for them
• Live Markets: Real-time betting on ongoing events
• Cash Out: Secure your profits or minimize losses before the event ends
• Market Depth: See available liquidity at different price points

How It Works

1. Choose your event and market
2. Select your stake and odds
3. Place your bet (back or lay)
4. Your bet is matched with another player
5. Watch the event and track your position
6. Collect your winnings after the event settles
